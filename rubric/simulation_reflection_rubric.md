# Simulation Reflection Grading Rubric

Total Points: 16

## Depth of Insight (4 points)

- **Exemplary (4):** Provides deep, thoughtful analysis supported by specific examples from the simulation. Makes insightful connections between the experience, nursing practice, and areas for professional growth.
- **Accomplished (3):** Provides clear and relevant reflection with meaningful connections to clinical learning and nursing practice.
- **Developing (2):** Provides general or surface-level reflection with limited connections to personal learning or nursing practice.
- **Beginning (1):** Provides minimal reflection with little insight or connection to nursing practice.
- **No Evidence (0):** Does not address the criterion or provides insufficient evidence of reflection.

## Self-Awareness (4 points)

- **Exemplary (4):** Demonstrates strong self-awareness by clearly identifying strengths and areas for growth and proposing specific, actionable next steps.
- **Accomplished (3):** Identifies strengths and areas for improvement and describes reasonable steps for future development.
- **Developing (2):** Vaguely identifies areas of performance or growth; suggestions for improvement lack specificity or clarity.
- **Beginning (1):** Provides little meaningful self-assessment or insight into personal performance.
- **No Evidence (0):** Does not address the criterion or provides insufficient evidence of self-awareness.

## Clinical Judgment & Decision-Making (4 points)

- **Exemplary (4):** Clearly explains the rationale for clinical decisions made during the simulation and supports decisions with relevant evidence-based principles or resources.
- **Accomplished (3):** Explains the reasoning behind most clinical decisions and includes some evidence-based support.
- **Developing (2):** Provides limited explanation of clinical decisions and demonstrates minimal use of evidence-based rationale.
- **Beginning (1):** Provides little or no explanation of clinical decisions, or decisions demonstrate significant clinical concerns.
- **No Evidence (0):** Does not address the criterion or provides insufficient evidence of clinical judgment or decision-making.

## Takeaways from Peer Feedback & Debriefing (4 points)

- **Exemplary (4):** Thoughtfully integrates specific feedback from peers, instructors, and the debriefing. Clearly explains how the feedback will inform future clinical practice.
- **Accomplished (3):** Identifies relevant feedback received and provides reasonable reflection on its value and application to future practice.
- **Developing (2):** Acknowledges feedback but provides limited reflection on its significance or impact on future practice.
- **Beginning (1):** Does not meaningfully address feedback or debriefing, or provides little reflection on how feedback could inform future practice.
- **No Evidence (0):** Does not address the criterion or provides insufficient evidence of engagement with feedback or debriefing.
